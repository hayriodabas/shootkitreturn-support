Pack out. Check back.

ShootKit Return is an offline equipment checklist for photographers and filmmakers. Build a shelf, prepare reusable kits, and check what actually comes back from each shoot.

In preparation for App Store review.

The first version is being tested. This page is not a claim that the app is currently available.

Two separate confirmations

Plan four batteries, pack three, return two: one remains outstanding. Intentionally left-behind equipment is not counted as missing.

Your data, on your iPhone

No ShootKit account. No advertising. Export CSV or a full backup to a destination you choose. Equipment checks depend on your confirmation; the app does not physically track gear.
