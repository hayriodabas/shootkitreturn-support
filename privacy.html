Privacy policy

Effective 13 October 2026. Developer: Hayri Odabaş.

Local equipment data

ShootKit Return stores equipment names, quantities, categories, bags, optional serial numbers and notes, kit templates, job manifests, check history and condition records locally on your iPhone. We do not operate a server that receives this data. The app has no account, advertising SDK, analytics SDK or tracking.

Camera

If you choose QR scanning, the app requests camera permission. Camera frames are processed on the device, not saved or uploaded. Permission can be changed in iPhone Settings. Manual checks remain available.

Files and backups

Files you explicitly export or share go to the service or recipient you choose and may contain sensitive information. Full backups include notes and serial numbers. Report notes are excluded by default and require an explicit choice. Device or iCloud backups may include app data according to your Apple settings.

Purchases

Apple handles payments, restoration and refunds. ShootKit reads verified StoreKit purchase entitlements to unlock Pro. We do not receive your payment card details.

Retention and deletion

Data stays on your device until you change it or remove the app. Archiving equipment preserves job history. Export a full backup before deleting the app. Copies in Files, cloud storage or device backups must be managed separately.

Support and this website

Support issues are hosted publicly on GitHub. Information you submit there is governed by GitHub’s privacy policy. This website uses GitHub Pages; GitHub may process technical request data as described in its privacy statement. We add no analytics or advertising cookies.

For privacy questions, use the support page without posting private equipment data.
