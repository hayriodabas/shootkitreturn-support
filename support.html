Field support

For help or a bug report, open a support issue. This requires a free GitHub account. Issues are public: do not include serial numbers, private notes, customer information or backups.

Getting started

Add equipment under Gear, optionally save a kit, and create a job. Check packed quantities or mark units intentionally left behind. Seal pack-out, then start return. Only equipment actually packed needs to come back.

Missing or damaged equipment

Missing units prevent normal completion. Use an explicit loss closure with a reason if equipment is lost. The physical return count does not change. Damaged or lost gear is unavailable for new jobs until you record repair or recovery. Reopen a closed job with a reason to correct its checks.

QR scanning

Camera permission is optional. Every check also works manually. ShootKit QR labels contain a version and a random local identifier, not personal details or a web link.

Back up and restore

Jobs → Settings → Create full backup. Save the file outside your iPhone. Restore replaces the entire workspace after validation; a local safety copy is made first. CSV and PDF reports are not full backups.

Free and Pro

Free includes 25 equipment entries, two kits and one active job, manual and QR checks, history, CSV export and backups. Pro is a one-time non-consumable upgrade for unlimited entries, kits and active jobs, multiple bags in a job, batch labels and custom print checklists. The app displays your local App Store price. Existing data remains available if Pro access changes.

Restore a purchase

Use the same Apple Account, open Settings → ShootKit Pro → Restore purchases. An internet connection is needed for store operations. Routine equipment checks work offline.
